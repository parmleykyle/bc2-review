Wall paint

This is the biggest surface in the room. These are the three colors already on your shortlist. The sheen is eggshell, so no gloss.

Designer suggests: Sherwin-Williams Alabaster SW 7008. A warm, soft white is the classic French country backdrop. It keeps the room bright and lets the pasture views and the black window frames be the color. Puritan Gray works better as an accent elsewhere.

Flooring

Polished concrete is logged for the main floors (still a 'want'). It's not decided whether the bedrooms get it too, so this asks that.

Designer suggests: Polished concrete, same as the main floors. One continuous floor keeps the house calm. A big vintage-style rug brings back the soft French country feel in the bedroom.

Designer suggests

Polished concrete, same as the main floors

Pair it with the area rug below for warmth underfoot. The sealer is still open: ECOS says the sealer you logged isn't meant for polished concrete, so confirm the product with them.

Ceiling light or fan

This is the room's main light and one of the brass repeats. A pasture house with the windows open in summer may also want a fan.

Designer suggests: Minka-Lavery Westchester County Flushmount, Farmhouse White with Gilded Gold Leaf. Candle-style gilded light is a French country signature. It sits flush, so it's safe on any ceiling, and the gold leaf is one of the room's brass repeats. Pick the fan instead if summer air matters more than looks.

Window treatments (black windows)

You look out on open pasture, so the goal is to frame the view, not cover it. Hang the treatment high and wide so it sits beside the glass, not over it, when open.

Designer suggests: IKEA DYTÅG linen curtains, white, 57×98" (1 pair) on a black HUGAD rod. Linen panels hung high and wide frame the pasture like a picture, and they're the most French country fabric. The black rod repeats the black window frames, the second black in the room.
