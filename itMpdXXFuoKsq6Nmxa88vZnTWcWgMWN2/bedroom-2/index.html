Flooring

Ceiling light or fan

This is the room's main light and one of the brass repeats. A pasture house with the windows open in summer may also want a fan.

Designer pick: Minka-Lavery Westchester County Flushmount, Farmhouse White with Gilded Gold Leaf. Candle-style gilded light is a French country signature. It sits flush, so it's safe on any ceiling, and the gold leaf is one of the room's brass repeats. Pick the fan instead if summer air matters more than looks.
